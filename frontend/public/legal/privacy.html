Privacy Policy (Datenschutzerklärung)

Last Updated: April 22, 2026

Summary: All-Chat collects only the information necessary to provide streaming overlay services. Tokens are encrypted, chat messages sent through All-Chat are automatically deleted after one hour, and we never sell your data.
Open Source & Transparency: All-Chat is FOSS licensed under AGPL-3.0. Review the source code at https://github.com/caesarakalaeii/all-chat.

1. Controller (Verantwortlicher, Art. 4 Nr. 7 DSGVO)

The person responsible for data processing is listed in the Impressum. For privacy inquiries contact all.chat.support@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Authentication Information

When you connect streaming platform accounts, we store:

  • Platform identifiers (user ID, username, display name)
  • Profile images provided by the platform
  • Encrypted OAuth access and refresh tokens
  • Token scopes and expiration dates

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) DSGVO – contract performance.

2.2 Chat Data

For active overlays we temporarily process chat messages, metadata, and author details.

Chat messages sent through All-Chat are automatically deleted after one hour. Messages displayed in overlays are streamed through memory and not persisted.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) DSGVO (service delivery) and Art. 6(1)(f) DSGVO (legitimate interest in abuse prevention).

2.3 Overlay Configuration

Overlay names, connected sources, themes, and filter settings.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) DSGVO.

2.4 Cross-Platform Viewer Identity

If you choose to link multiple platform accounts as a viewer, we create a unified viewer profile. This is opt-in only.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) DSGVO – consent.

2.5 Usage & Log Data

  • Anonymised IP address (last octet zeroed), browser user agent, basic request info
  • API usage metrics and error traces (retained up to 90 days)

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) DSGVO – legitimate interest in security.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate against platforms you authorize
  • Fetch and normalize live chat into a single feed
  • Render overlays, emote lookups, and filters
  • Monitor reliability and detect abuse

4. Data Storage & Security

  • PostgreSQL (account data, encrypted OAuth tokens)
  • Redis (ephemeral sessions, message fan-out)
  • AES-GCM encryption for tokens at rest
  • HTTPS at ingress for external traffic; internal services isolated via Kubernetes network policies

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

5.1 Streaming Platform APIs

Twitch, YouTube, TikTok, Kick, Discord, 7TV, BTTV, FFZ – as needed for the service.

5.2 Fonts (Self-Hosted)

Typography assets originally distributed by Google Fonts are self-hosted on our infrastructure. Interface fonts are bundled at build time; overlay-selectable fonts are served through a server-side proxy at /api/fonts/*. Your browser only connects to the All-Chat origin; no IP address, user agent, or request metadata is transmitted to Google when fonts are loaded (LG München I, 20 January 2022, Az. 3 O 17493/20).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) DSGVO. Font files are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 or Apache 2.0.

5.3 Third-Party Frontend Resources

Overlay and dashboard pages may load resources that transmit your IP address to:

  • UI Avatars (ui-avatars.com) – fallback avatar images
  • GitHub API (api.github.com) – theme marketplace

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) DSGVO.

5.4 No Data Sales

We never sell or rent your data.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: until you delete your account
  • OAuth tokens: deleted on disconnect, expiry (+7 days), or account deletion
  • Chat messages (sent through All-Chat): automatically deleted after 1 hour
  • Chat messages (overlay display): memory only, not persisted
  • Usage logs: up to 90 days
  • YouTube quota audit: 30 days

7. Your Rights (Art. 15–21 DSGVO)

  • Access (Art. 15)
  • Rectification (Art. 16)
  • Erasure (Art. 17) – delete account via Settings or contact us
  • Restriction (Art. 18)
  • Data portability (Art. 20) – export via Settings
  • Objection (Art. 21)
  • Withdraw consent – disconnect platforms at any time

Contact: all.chat.support@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

8. Cookies & Browser Storage

We use browser localStorage (not cookies) for authentication tokens and preferences. No advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics. Fonts are self-hosted (Section 5.2) and do not set cookies; the third-party resources listed in Section 5.3 may set their own cookies when loaded.

9. Children's Privacy

Not intended for users under 16. Data of minors will be deleted immediately.

10. International Transfers

Platform integrations involve transfers to Twitch (US), Google (US), TikTok (various), Kick (AU). The GitHub API (theme marketplace) may also involve transfers to the US. Fonts are self-hosted and do not involve any third-country transfer when loaded. Legal basis: Art. 49(1)(b) DSGVO.

Changes posted here with updated date. Significant changes announced in the dashboard.
