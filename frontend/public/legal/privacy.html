Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 12, 2026

Summary: All-Chat collects only the information necessary to provide streaming overlay services. We connect to your streaming platform accounts (Twitch, YouTube, TikTok, Kick, Discord) to display chat messages. We do not sell your data to third parties.
Open Source & Transparency: All-Chat is Free and Open Source Software (FOSS) licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0). You can review the complete source code, including how we handle your data, at: https://github.com/caesarakalaeii/all-chat

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Authentication Information

When you connect streaming platform accounts, we collect:

  • Platform user ID and username
  • Display name and avatar URL
  • OAuth access tokens (encrypted and stored securely)
  • OAuth refresh tokens (encrypted and stored securely)
  • Token expiration dates

1.2 Chat Data

To provide overlay services, we process:

  • Chat messages from connected channels/streams
  • Message metadata (timestamps, user badges, emotes)
  • User information from chat messages (usernames, display names, avatars)

Important: Chat messages are processed in real-time and are not permanently stored. Messages are temporarily cached in memory for active overlay sessions only.

1.3 Overlay Configuration

We store your overlay settings, including:

  • Overlay names and IDs
  • Connected chat sources (platform and channel mappings)
  • Display preferences (CSS styles, filters)

1.4 Usage Data

We automatically collect:

  • Log data (IP addresses, browser type, timestamps)
  • Service usage metrics (API calls, connection status)
  • Error logs and diagnostic information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the All-Chat service
  • Authenticate you with streaming platforms
  • Fetch and display chat messages from connected sources
  • Process and enrich messages with emotes
  • Monitor service performance and troubleshoot issues
  • Improve and optimize the service
  • Communicate with you about service updates

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Storage Location

Your data is stored in:

  • PostgreSQL database (account information, overlay configurations)
  • Redis cache (temporary session data, active connections)

3.2 Security Measures

We implement security measures including:

  • AES-GCM encryption of OAuth tokens at rest
  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for data in transit
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security updates and patches
  • Monitoring and logging of access attempts

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

4.1 Third-Party Platform APIs

We interact with third-party APIs:

  • Twitch: IRC connection, Helix API, user authentication
  • YouTube: Internal APIs (InnerTube), OAuth authentication
  • TikTok: Unofficial reverse-engineered WebSocket library (beta)
  • Kick: Pusher WebSocket API
  • Discord: Gateway API, bot integration
  • 7TV, BTTV, FFZ: Emote data enrichment

Your use of these integrations is subject to the respective platform's privacy policies.

4.2 No Data Sales

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, such as:

  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Protection of our rights and property
  • Prevention of fraud or security issues
  • Response to lawful requests from authorities

5. Data Retention

We retain your information as follows:

  • Account data: Until you delete your account
  • OAuth tokens: Until you disconnect the platform or tokens expire
  • Chat messages: Not stored permanently; only processed in real-time
  • Usage logs: Retained for up to 90 days for debugging and analytics

13. Platform-Specific Privacy Notes

TikTok Integration

When you add a TikTok source to your overlay:

  • We connect to TikTok Live streams using an unofficial, reverse-engineered WebSocket library (beta)
  • No OAuth authentication or TikTok account connection is required
  • We receive real-time chat messages during live streams
  • We do not access TikTok videos, followers, or other personal content beyond live chat
  • You can stop data collection by removing the TikTok source from your overlay in All-Chat settings
  • This integration may break without notice if TikTok changes its internal APIs

Twitch Integration

When you connect your Twitch account:

  • We connect to Twitch IRC to receive chat messages
  • We use the Twitch Helix API for user authentication and EventSub webhooks
  • We do not access your channel analytics, subscriber information, or payment data

YouTube Integration

When you connect your YouTube account:

  • We use YouTube's internal APIs (InnerTube) to fetch live chat messages
  • We use YouTube OAuth for account authentication
  • We do not access your video content, channel analytics, or subscriber data
  • Your use of All-Chat's YouTube integration is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy

Revoking YouTube Access: You can revoke All-Chat's access to your YouTube data at any time by:

  • Visiting the Google security settings page and removing All-Chat from your connected applications
  • Or by disconnecting YouTube from the All-Chat Settings page

Deleting YouTube Data: To delete your YouTube-related data from All-Chat, you can disconnect your YouTube account from the Settings page. This will delete your stored OAuth tokens. Chat messages are not permanently stored and are only processed in real-time.

Kick Integration

When you add a Kick source to your overlay:

  • We connect to Kick chat channels via the Pusher WebSocket API
  • We receive real-time chat messages from the specified channels
  • We do not access your Kick account data, channel analytics, or subscriber information
  • You can stop data collection by removing the Kick source from your overlay

Discord Integration

When you add a Discord source to your overlay:

  • We connect to Discord channels via the Discord Gateway API using a bot
  • We receive real-time messages from configured Discord channels
  • We do not access your direct messages, server settings, or member lists beyond what is needed for chat relay
  • You can stop data collection by removing the Discord source from your overlay or removing the bot from your server
