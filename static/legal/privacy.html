Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2025

Summary: All-Chat collects only the information necessary to provide streaming overlay services. We connect to your streaming platform accounts (Twitch, YouTube, TikTok, Kick) to display chat messages. We do not sell your data to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Authentication Information

When you connect streaming platform accounts, we collect:

  • Platform user ID and username
  • Display name and avatar URL
  • OAuth access tokens (encrypted and stored securely)
  • OAuth refresh tokens (encrypted and stored securely)
  • Token expiration dates

1.2 Chat Data

To provide overlay services, we process:

  • Chat messages from connected channels/streams
  • Message metadata (timestamps, user badges, emotes)
  • User information from chat messages (usernames, display names, avatars)

Important: Chat messages are processed in real-time and are not permanently stored. Messages are temporarily cached in memory for active overlay sessions only.

1.3 Overlay Configuration

We store your overlay settings, including:

  • Overlay names and IDs
  • Connected chat sources (platform and channel mappings)
  • Display preferences (CSS styles, filters)

1.4 Usage Data

We automatically collect:

  • Log data (IP addresses, browser type, timestamps)
  • Service usage metrics (API calls, connection status)
  • Error logs and diagnostic information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the All-Chat service
  • Authenticate you with streaming platforms
  • Fetch and display chat messages from connected sources
  • Process and enrich messages with emotes
  • Monitor service performance and troubleshoot issues
  • Improve and optimize the service
  • Communicate with you about service updates

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Storage Location

Your data is stored in:

  • PostgreSQL database (account information, overlay configurations)
  • Redis cache (temporary session data, active connections)

3.2 Security Measures

We implement security measures including:

  • Encryption of OAuth tokens at rest
  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for data in transit
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security updates and patches
  • Monitoring and logging of access attempts

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

4.1 Third-Party Platform APIs

We interact with third-party APIs:

  • Twitch: IRC connection, user authentication
  • YouTube: Live Chat API, OAuth authentication
  • TikTok: Live API, OAuth authentication (planned)
  • Kick: API integration (planned)
  • 7TV, BTTV, FFZ: Emote data enrichment

Your use of these integrations is subject to the respective platform's privacy policies.

4.2 No Data Sales

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, such as:

  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Protection of our rights and property
  • Prevention of fraud or security issues
  • Response to lawful requests from authorities

5. Data Retention

We retain your information as follows:

  • Account data: Until you delete your account
  • OAuth tokens: Until you disconnect the platform or tokens expire
  • Chat messages: Not stored permanently; only processed in real-time
  • Usage logs: Retained for up to 90 days for debugging and analytics

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Disconnect: Revoke platform authorizations at any time
  • Export: Download your overlay configurations

To exercise these rights, contact us at allchat@caes.ar

7. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal cookies and tracking:

  • Session cookies: For authentication and session management
  • WebSocket connections: For real-time message delivery

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or trackers.

8. Children's Privacy

All-Chat is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we discover we have collected such information, we will delete it immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. By using the Service, you consent to such transfers.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Sending an email notification (for significant changes)

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: allchat@caes.ar

12. Platform-Specific Privacy Notes

TikTok Integration

When you connect your TikTok account:

  • We request permissions to access your live stream chat data
  • We receive real-time chat messages during your live streams
  • We do not access your TikTok videos, followers, or other personal content beyond live chat
  • You can revoke access at any time through TikTok's app settings or All-Chat settings

Twitch Integration

When you connect your Twitch account:

  • We connect to Twitch IRC to receive chat messages
  • We do not access your channel analytics, subscriber information, or payment data

YouTube Integration

When you connect your YouTube account:

  • We use YouTube Live Chat API to fetch chat messages
  • We do not access your video content, channel analytics, or subscriber data
  • API usage is subject to YouTube's quota limits
